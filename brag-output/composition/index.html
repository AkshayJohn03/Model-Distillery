training plan — priced before you rent

hand-computed fixture ✓ to 1e-9
QLoRA
Train a small adapter on a compressed base model — one GPU suffices.
LoRA adapter
r=16 on q/k/v/o — about 0.25% of the parameters, all of the learning.
NF4
4-bit NormalFloat — a codebook shaped like trained weights.
double quantization
Even the quantization constants get quantized — ~0.4 bits per param saved.
logit

soft labels
The teacher's full probabilities — how sure to be, not just what to say.
KL divergence
The distance between two belief distributions — zero when identical.
